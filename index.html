Apps made by doctors, for doctors

Phone apps that make the ward day easier.

HBJLabs builds simple, offline tools for South African doctors. They are made by doctors who work in the public sector, from what we need on the ward every day. Built around POPIA: no servers, and patient information never leaves your phone unless you send it.

WardCalc

Bedside calculators and medicine doses from the South African Standard Treatment Guidelines (Adult and Paediatric Hospital level and Primary Healthcare), worked out from one weight. Works offline. Stores no patient information. Free.

FreeComing soon to Google Play · Privacy notice

WardBoard

Your ward round on one board: patients by ward and bed, jobs with reminders, results by date, and handover to a colleague's phone by QR code. Everything stays on your phone behind a PIN.

Coming soon to Google Play · Privacy notice

LabScan

For clinicians with their own NHLS TrakCare access: scan the specimen barcode and see the results clearly on your phone, abnormal first, with trends and alerts when pending results arrive.

In testing · Privacy notice

No servers

We run no servers and keep no copy of your patients' information. It stays on your phone.

Doctor to doctor

Information moves only when you choose: by QR code, between our apps on your phone, or through your phone's share menu.

Minimum data

Each step sends only what the next one needs. WardCalc never sees a name at all.
