CallRoster helps a department make its call roster, and lets each doctor see their own roster. It handles no patient information.

What CallRoster stores

Only on your phone: the setup you enter (department, the doctors’ surnames, shifts, hours and rules, firms, wards and clinics), the requests file you import from your department’s form, the leave dates, days off and on-call days you type in, the rosters it makes, and the rosters your rep sends you with the surname you chose. Colleagues’ names and leave are personal information, so keep only what the roster needs.

What CallRoster sends

Nothing to us. CallRoster has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server.

  • You choose the requests file to import from your phone; the app only reads it.
  • “Save roster and tallies” writes an Excel file to a folder you choose; you decide where it goes from there.
  • “Send to the group” saves a roster file for you to share (for example on your group’s WhatsApp). It holds the department, the shifts and their hours, and who works when and where, by surname. It holds no leave dates, requests or contact details: leave shows only as “Off”.
  • Shift reminders are notifications set on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • If you choose to put your shifts in your calendar, CallRoster adds them to a “CallRoster” calendar on your phone. It looks only for that calendar and changes only the events it put there; your other events stay unread and untouched.

Purchases

Paid roster months and CallRoster Plus are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we receive no card or bank details, and Google’s privacy policy applies to the purchase.

App updates

Updates to the app are downloaded from Expo, the service we use to deliver them. This sends the app’s version, your phone type and a random number that identifies the installation (not you), and nothing from your rosters.

Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you may ask what information we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted. Because we hold none on our side, the information in the app is in your own hands: you can delete a project in the app at any time, or uninstall it. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za). Questions: info@hbjlabs.co.za.

Changes

If this notice changes we will update it here and change the date above.
