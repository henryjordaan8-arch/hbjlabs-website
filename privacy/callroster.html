CallRoster helps a department make its calls roster. It handles no patient information.

What CallRoster stores

Only on your phone: the setup you enter (department, the doctors’ surnames, shifts, hours and rules, firms), the requests file you import from your department’s form, the leave dates and days off you type in, and the rosters it makes. Colleagues’ names and leave are personal information, so keep only what the roster needs.

What CallRoster sends

Nothing to us. CallRoster has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server.

  • You choose the requests file to import from your phone; the app only reads it.
  • “Save the roster” and “Save the tallies” write Excel files to a folder you choose; you decide where they go from there.

App updates

Updates to the app are downloaded from Expo, the service we use to deliver them. This sends the app’s version and your phone type, not personal information.

Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you may ask what information we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted. Because we hold none on our side, the information in the app is in your own hands: you can delete a project in the app at any time, or uninstall it. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za). Questions: info@hbjlabs.co.za.

Changes

If this notice changes we will update it here and change the date above.
