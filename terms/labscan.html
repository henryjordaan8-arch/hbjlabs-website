1. Who LabScan is for

LabScan is for registered health practitioners in South Africa who have their own authorised access to the NHLS results website (TrakCare). It is not a medical device: it does not interpret results, diagnose or give advice, and it does not replace your clinical judgement or your hospital’s protocols. 2. Always check against the official report

LabScan reads results automatically from NHLS web pages and reports, and that reading can go wrong: a value can be missed, misread or filed under the wrong test. Arrows, alert marks, “pending” and other markings are reminders only. Always confirm a result against the official NHLS report before acting on it.
