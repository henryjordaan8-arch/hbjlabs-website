1. Who WardBoard is for

WardBoard is for qualified health professionals and students working under supervision. It is a personal organiser for your own ward work: a list of your patients, your jobs and your notes. It is not a medical record and does not replace your hospital’s records, handover procedures or protocols, or your clinical judgement. It does not give medical advice or make decisions about a patient. You remain responsible for every decision you make for a patient.

2. Patient information is your responsibility

The patient information you enter is personal and health information. As the health professional using WardBoard you are responsible for it under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), your professional rules and your hospital’s policies. In particular:

  • Use WardBoard only for patients in your care, and only if your hospital allows it.
  • Keep your phone and the app locked (WardBoard asks for a PIN, and blocks screenshots).
  • Share ward work (by QR code or passcode-locked email) only with colleagues involved in the patients’ care, and give an email’s passcode separately from the email.
  • Remove patients you no longer care for, and erase everything (Settings) before you give away, sell or hand in your phone.

3. Keep your own records

WardBoard keeps its data only on your phone, with no cloud copy. If the phone is lost, broken or reset, or the app is removed, what is in WardBoard is gone and we cannot recover it. Reminders depend on your phone’s notification and battery settings and may arrive late or not at all. Don’t rely on WardBoard alone for anything a patient’s safety depends on.

4. Results from other apps

Results and scores that arrive from LabScan or WardCalc are shown for you to check, and saved only once you confirm the patient. Check that each one belongs to the right patient and matches the source before you act on it.
